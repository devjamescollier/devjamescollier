### Hardware Projects

I am a hardware developer first. I enjoy PCB design and integrating electronic systems with embedded firmware. Most of my projects center around signal generation, data acquisition, and power systems!

#### ([STM32-Based Programmable Waveform Generator](https://github.com/devjamescollier/Dual-Channel_Embedded_Power_Supply_Board.git))

- A USB-powered function generator built around the **STM32C031K6T6** and **MCP4901 DAC**. The board generates sine, triangle, and square waves with adjustable amplitude, onboard controls, status indicators, and an SWD programming interface.

`KiCad EDA` `STM32` `Embedded C` `Signals and Systems` `Analog Design`

#### [Dual-Channel Embedded Power Control Board](https://github.com/devjamescollier/Dual-Channel_Embedded_Power_Supply_Board)

- A compact dual-output DC-DC power board based on two **Texas Instruments TPS54331** buck converters. It converts a single battery input into regulated **+5 V** and **+3.3 V** rails for embedded systems.

`KiCad EDA` `LTspice` `Simulink` `DC-DC Conversion` `Power Electronics`

#### [Embedded Avionics and Sensor Data Acquisition PCB](LINK)

- An embedded avionics platform for collecting, conditioning, and processing sensor data. Designed around reliable power distribution, mixed-signal acquisition, and modular interfaces for future flight-system integration.

`Altium Designer` `ESP32` `Embedded C` `Data Acquisition` `Avionics`

## Technology Stack

For hardware development, I use **Altium Designer** and **KiCad EDA** for schematic capture and PCB layout, along with **LTspice** and **Simulink** for modeling and simulation. I also work with **STMCubeIDE** for configuring my microcontrollers, firmware development, and debugging.

For software development, I work mostly in low-level **C** and **C++** with **Python** and **MATLAB** for testing, analysis, and simulation as well as **Bash** for scripting. I primarily work with **STM32**, **ESP32**, and **ATmega328P** for embedded development. I use **Verilog** and **SystemVerilog** for digital hardware design. **Neovim** is my primary text editor.
